`market/city.md` carries Marcus Milan's July rule: top-3 profiles on 300+ reviews, skip the city and
spread the reviews over more profiles. Two months later he scores Bellevue, WA, whose plumbing top runs
in the thousands of reviews, and still ranks plumbing first there and would be open to it. One speaker
against his own earlier word: a revision.

## Conclusion

The later word reads as a score, not a cutoff: heavy competition is one count of five, and a trade strong
on the others can outrank a city's review counts. That matches the conclusion `market/city.md` already
stands on, the owner's: what a top-3 place costs in reviews, not a review cutoff. So nothing in the
manual stood on the July skip rule; it stays as his evidence, with the September scoring under it.

Where it is not conclusive: "open to it" is not "would launch there". If he launches only below 300,
the July rule stands for him as a launch rule, and the scorecard only orders trades.

His density floor is weighed here but not revised by this video: Bellevue at ~4,500 is below his ~6,000,
yet a control city for scoring trades is not a city he says he would launch in. His later word on density
belongs here.
