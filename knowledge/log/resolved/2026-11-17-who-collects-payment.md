## Conclusion

Not three positions but one rule with a threshold. Until ~$3–4k a month the contractor collects, under
his own name: the liability for the job is his, and he sells harder. Past that, set the company up and
collect ourselves, at which point the VA is the one who takes the payment. Eric collecting from the start
is the same rule run with more appetite for risk, and he reports no trouble from it.

Marcus Milan never takes collection over, at ~$30k a month. So the threshold is not where collecting
ourselves becomes necessary: Eric's "super safe" is about not collecting under our name before the company
exists. Past it the two ways coexist. The contractor collecting keeps the liability his and the upsell
incentive, and our cut comes after, on his word, checked by calling his customers; collecting ourselves
holds the money and puts the job under our brand. What would decide between them: how often a cut comes
in short or late against what a claim on a job under our name costs. Nobody has given either.
