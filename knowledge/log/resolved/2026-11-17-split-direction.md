## Conclusion

In this group "60/40" is 60 to the contractor and 40 to us, of profit. Every source that names a side
says so, and nobody on any call describes taking 60. The suggested bullet read the 2026-09-18 line the
other way with nothing behind it; it is removed. The owner's bullet stands as written: 40 to us from a
master plumber was the surprise, since a master was expected to hold out for 70/30.
