## Conclusion

Scoped by niche and by suite, they need not conflict. Matt Gazda's NAP rule is "never shared with another
profile in its niche", and he is unsure how the address trip works; Eric's second profile is another niche,
and the member's three were separate suites, so the full NAP differs each time. The address bullet now sits
under a `(?)` conclusion: a second profile on the same address and in the same niche can trip suspension;
a different niche, or its own suite, on one address ranks for other categories and searches. What would
decide it: a count of same-address profiles suspended, split by same niche and different niche.
