## Conclusion

Not two rules: Eric pays for friends and family, and is strict that each be a person of their own. What
he warns of is one person behind several reviews, which his own video has deleted. So friends and family
are taken only on their own phone and gmail, and a respondent sending five or six in minutes is the case
he warns of. The members' batches are days old, and deletions land 1–2 weeks on; John's half deleted
may be the same thing.

What would decide it: those batches checked two weeks on, and John's deleted half traced to who left
them.
