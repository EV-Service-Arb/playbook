## Conclusion

Scoped by trade, not a contradiction. The visit and the in-house price are for plumbing and HVAC, where
nobody can price the job unseen; detailing and house cleaning are low-commitment work whose price is
named and booked on the call. Eric's own September "medium pricing" stands across trades and cleaning's
median fits it; detailing's upper side is the exception for a ticket so low that the low end leaves
nothing, and goes into the bullet. Devin Grandt's "leaning low" stays as his evidence.

What would reopen it: a detailer at the low end of his area winning more on volume than the upper side
does per job.
