# an untouched profile: does it keep its leads?

`reviews.md` holds that velocity counts on top of count: "500 reviews with the last one six months ago
does little for you" (Zach Peterson), with Derick Parsons and John Osayi on weekly reviews. Eric's
November video shows a profile whose reviews all went on over a few days, untouched for over half a
year, still bringing interactions, and says built profiles keep generating leads "literally forever",
while his own October numbers show it fading.

## Conclusion

Velocity stands. Eric's profile is the case the rule is about, and his own numbers bear it out more than
they cut against it: a profile built once kept bringing leads for most of a year, then faded in October,
which is when many members' profiles fell out of the top 3 at once and Eric and James McBride read google
as weighing recent activity more. So the bullet keeps "reviews every week" and says what an untouched
profile does: it holds for months, then fades. His "literally forever" is not carried: his October
numbers are against it.

What is not settled: whether the Newmarket profile faded from its age without new reviews or from the
October re-ranking alone. A profile kept on weekly reviews through October, beside one left alone, would
tell them apart.
