`market/niche.md` has us master one trade before adding another, on Eric's word to a member already on
plumbing. On the 2026-11-19 call Connor, "deferring to Eric", advises running a small quick-cash trade
next to the big niche; Everett, on the same call, holds his second trade back until his HVAC is

## Conclusion

Not a contradiction once each is scoped by what its first trade is. Eric's rule was given for plumbing,
paid within a day and busy year round: it has no gap a second trade would fill, so the second only adds
a contractor, a script and a VA's training. Connor's big niche is landscaping, whose jobs close over weeks
of quotes: the quick-cash trade carries the wait, as Zach Peterson's water softener carries a dead season.
So `market/niche.md` keeps "master one trade" for a trade busy year round and paid within a day, adds
Everett's step to it (widen the region with the same trade first), and gains "a high-ticket trade that
closes slowly takes a low-ticket one paid within the week beside it", beside the dead-season one.

What would reopen it: an operator on a fast trade whose second trade, added early, paid without cost to
the first; or Connor's numbers showing the detailing cash did not keep the landscaping going.
