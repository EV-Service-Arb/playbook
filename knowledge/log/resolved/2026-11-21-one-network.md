## Conclusion

Muhammad's is a prediction (1σ); Everett's is a result, a month and a half past the 1–2 week deletion
delay. A university's network carries thousands of real people, and as common knowledge goes such a
shared address is a weak signal on its own. What Muhammad's IP log does catch is one person posting as
many, which is how his $500 was lost. So: log scan IPs to catch repeats; a campus network shared by many
real students has not taken reviews down.

Not settled: Everett's own "off wifi" earlier in the call sits against his "never asked anyone off the
campus wifi", which the digest may have misheard. What would decide it: whether Everett's campus-network
reviews still stand after several months.
