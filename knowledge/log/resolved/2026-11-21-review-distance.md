# how far a reviewer can be: Eric's 40 miles, or a dining hall 120 miles off

`reviews/sourcing/remote.md` keeps reviews near the profile, on Eric's rule, his 40 miles called
conservative. On the 2026-11-19 call Everett, a member, reports dining-hall reviews on a profile 120 miles
away that all stuck.

## Conclusion

Scoped by who the reviewer is, as far as the evidence goes. Every far-off review on record that stuck was
a real person asked in person, on their own account: Everett's students, Ilya's mall-goers. The far-off
ones on record that were deleted were Eric's "random guy in India", an online reviewer. So the rule stays
for reviews gathered online, and real people asked in person are added as having stuck from much farther,
on a new profile too, against the member's 6–12 months.

Not conclusive: it is one member's profiles over a month and a half, and he calls it luck himself. It also
sits beside Ilya's philippine reviews, which Eric's "bar a couple of methods" may already mean. What
would decide it: a stick rate for in-person reviews placed past 40 miles over more profiles and a longer
run, and whether Eric's deleted far-off reviews were ever in-person ones.
