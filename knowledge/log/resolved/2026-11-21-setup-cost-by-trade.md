# a profile's setup cost by trade

`market/niche.md`'s ticket bullet held that a profile costs the same to set up whatever the trade, so
past the first months a higher ticket outweighs the ops load. Marcus Milan, scoring three trades, says
how easily a profile gets up counts beside the metrics, and that pest control's get up much easier than
plumbing's. Two speakers, one premise: a contradiction on the premise, not on the conclusion.

## Conclusion

The premise does not hold: the manual itself has plumbing verifying mostly by video in the States,
pest control without one, detailing instantly on an address. What it takes to get a profile up differs
by trade, and Eric judges a trade on it first. The conclusion stands without the premise: past the first
months a higher ticket outweighs the ops load of many small jobs. Ilya weighs ops load, which neither
Marcus nor the verification evidence touches. The setup cost is now weighed beside the ticket, and a trade
whose profiles get up easier can stand level with a higher-ticket one, as pest control does with plumbing
for Marcus; a month on he counts easier verification among low ticket's pluses, which leaves the conclusion
as it is: he does not weigh the ops load either. Ilya's reason stays as he gave it, under his evidence.

What would decide further: how many profiles each trade loses before one verifies, in our own runs.
Nobody has given that per trade.
