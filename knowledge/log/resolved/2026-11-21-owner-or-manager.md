## Conclusion

Two methods with different trade-offs, both in use; neither is evicted. Manager access suits a profile
that stays on someone else's account and phone, which was Eric's case in 2025-12 ("we do a lot on other
people's phones"), and Valera's reason, one admin across many accounts read as less suspicious than one
owner, stands beside it. Primary ownership on our own gmail suits a profile handed over for good: Eric's
2026-11-13 practice (transfer at once, 20 in one personal gmail), the friend route, Everett's 16. Manager
access stays the first step on a bought gmail before its password changes.

Applied: `profile/creation.md` `## access` opens with a `(?)` conclusion naming both, Eric's 2026-11-13
lines under it; the owner's bullets stay as written. `infra.md`'s "2–3 at most when we sit on the
profiles as owner" is Valera's range and stays, with Eric's and Everett's owner-held counts beside it as
the evidence against. What decides it: suspensions of owner-held against manager-held profiles over half
a year; Everett's is one operator and one month.
