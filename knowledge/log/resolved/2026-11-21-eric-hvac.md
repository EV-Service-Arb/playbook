# Eric on HVAC: competition and verification

In June Eric ranked HVAC below plumbing for its competition: very competitive, an area to rank it in hard
to find, plumbing's competition a little lower. At the end of July he calls it slept on: few lead-gen
operators run it, the owners holding its top profiles carry no keywords, and plumbing is the trade crowded
with people running his model. The same July video praises HVAC for verifying by text, call or email code,
which he advises against in October. One speaker against his own earlier word: a revision.

## Conclusion

The July word stands on competition: few lead-gen operators run HVAC, so a keyworded address profile
ranks quickly against owners who don't market, and plumbing is where lead-gen operators crowd. His June
"very competitive, hard to find an area" stays under `### other` on `market/niches/hvac.md`; the
Los Angeles counts stay as evidence that a big city can still be held on reviews, which the July word does
not deny. His June and July agree that HVAC goes on an address. His October list of the best trades,
HVAC among them, sides with July.

On verification the October word stands: the codes google offers HVAC are a fact about the trade, but
taking them is the route he advises against. So the leniency is kept as what google offers, not as a
reason to pick HVAC.

What would reopen it: one city's numbers. The July claim rests on Toronto and on two student profiles
in Ontario; a US metro whose top HVAC profiles are keyworded lead-gen profiles would put it back to June.
