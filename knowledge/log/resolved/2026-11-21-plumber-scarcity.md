## Conclusion

Both hold, scoped by the city: a plumber takes our leads where he is short of work, which is most cities,
and not where plumbers have more than they can take, as in Montreal, where two members report the same.
So "easy to find in most cities", and the per-city check is the one `market.md` already runs before the
trade is chosen: count the contractors answering `looking for` posts in the area's groups. Montreal's
evidence sits on `regions/canada.md` under quebec, beside the NEQ, its other reason a profile there earns
less than its leads.

What would decide further: whether Montreal is short of plumbers or only of ones who take a stranger's
leads; Prabh's VA calling more of them, or his move to another city, is the next data point.
