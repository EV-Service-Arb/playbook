## Conclusion

Both agree on codes. On instant they want different things: Eric a profile live today, Matt one standing months on. Eric's own record backs video for longevity, and nobody reports instant-verified profiles lasting. So a profile meant to last goes by video, live call or address; instant stays the route for speed, at a cost nobody has counted. What would decide it: the share of instant-verified profiles suspended within 6 months, against video-verified ones.
