## Conclusion

Plumbing stays the start. The member's word is one unsourced 1σ aside from someone not running
arbitrage, against Eric's and Marcus Milan's repeated advice and against members' results on it in
2026-10 and 2026-11. What it adds is already in the manual in weaker form: plumbing is crowded with
lead-gen operators and its competition leaves fewer great cities. So it is carried as scope, not as a
verdict: in a major US city the broad plumbing term is held, and the trade is taken in a smaller city
(Vaughan against Mississauga) or on a sub-service whose own name is unworked, as Eric's Dallas drain
cleaning read shows, the reason his November start is drain cleaning under plumbing. The evidence sits on
`market/niches/plumbing.md` `## the market`; the starting-trade bullet on `market/niche.md` carries the
scope.

What would decide it: the top three's review counts for `plumber <city>` and `drain cleaning <city>`
across a sample of major US cities, read the way `market/city.md` reads a city.
