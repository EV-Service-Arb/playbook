the heaviest operations. On the 2026-11-05 call Derick Parsons runs 19 profiles, all cleaning, toward a
$20k month. Read as a contradiction only if his month says cleaning is where to start.

## Conclusion

No contradiction: Derick does not say where to start, and the call does not say whether he started on
cleaning or came in with operations, as Ilya did. Whether his $20k is revenue or what he keeps is not
said either; "16–17,000 across everything" reads as the month's total. What his month does show is
that cleaning pays on profile count, the month growing with the profiles, at over half a job kept where the
cleaner names her price. That goes on the page as a conclusion of its own, under `## economics`, and his
$250 of $500 beside Eric's $150–200 a call, both kept. The start stays off cleaning: the ticket per profile
set up is still half plumbing's, Houston needs 150+ reviews where a plumbing city can take a few dozen, and he
runs every call and payment himself at 19 profiles.

What would reopen it: Derick's profit for the month, against what 19 plumbing profiles clear, and the
hours he spends on calls and payments.
