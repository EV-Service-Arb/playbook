## Conclusion

Leaving: not a contradiction once scoped. `PP`'s excuses cover what can be excused, and he ran them;
Devin's case is an ask that can neither be shown nor excused, where staying on only loses the call. So
excuse and stay on; an ask with neither an answer nor an excuse ends the call, to come back fixed on
another, as the course does for a strict rep.

When to call: not settled. `PP`'s morning comes off his own calls; Devin's night is advice from one who
doesn't run live. `PP`'s stands, Devin's beside it. What would decide it: approvals counted by the hour
of the call.
