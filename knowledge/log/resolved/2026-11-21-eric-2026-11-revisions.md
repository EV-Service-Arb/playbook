## Conclusion

The later word stands each time. `profile/verification.md` now starts a profile as a service area and
moves it to the address later, his September answer kept beneath as revised; `verification/sab_to_pab.md`
leads with the route. `reviews/sourcing/remote.md` keeps reviews local, with his 40 miles called
conservative, and Ilya's ~1h restatement dated to September. `reviews/deletion.md` puts completely
different people on each profile, an account reused on a second only after five days to a week, his
September "never" kept beneath. None of the three is a reversal: each loosens a rule he still
prefers to keep.

Two weeks on, on the 2026-11-19 call, he turned the first again: there is no good way to add an address to
a service-area profile now, and an instant verification takes an address as readily as a service area,
so the address goes on from the start. The later word stands. `profile/verification.md` now puts the
address on first, his 2026-11-05 answer kept beneath as revised; `verification/sab_to_pab.md` is for a
profile already up as a service area: add the address, and if that fails, verify a new one instantly.
The member's "video-verified survives the switch better" stays as the case for whoever still converts.

What would reopen it: a stick rate for reviews from past 40 miles, or a count of address-first
profiles passing against service-area-first ones.
