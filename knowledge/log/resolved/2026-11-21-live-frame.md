## Conclusion

Both happen: a frame has passed live, and been rejected and grilled live, the rep deciding. Eric's
approvals are the newest and the most, and he names the frame as what they showed; the rejections are
older, and the October member passed once before he was grilled. So a frame can pass from a home office,
mounted firmly, a sign on the door beside it, and a spare ticket open for the rep who calls it paper.
On a recorded video, where no rep tugs it, a frame usually passes: that stays on `video.md`.

What would decide it: members' live calls counted, frame against a mounted sign, since November.
