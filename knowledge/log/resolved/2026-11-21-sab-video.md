## Conclusion

Scoped, they agree. A service-area video needs no storefront or signage on a door, which is what makes
it easier, but it is shot inside the area it claims: easier for whoever is there, Eric's own exception,
harder from outside it, where faking the surroundings is what fails, as Gabe Jacobs' edits did. The
member gives no case of his own. The one bullet says so, both beneath it.

What would decide it further: a member shooting service-area videos from outside the area they claim.
