Eric says HVAC is not seasonal, no struggle for jobs in winter or summer. `Kit Volcano` says HVAC goes
quiet in mild weather, windows open and nothing breaking. Two speakers.

## Conclusion

Both hold, and they name different months. Eric's two seasons are the extremes, heating in one and
cooling in the other; Kit Volcano's is the mild weather between them. That matches how the trade is known
to run: busy in winter and summer, slow in the spring and autumn shoulders. So HVAC has no dead half of the
year, as pool cleaning can, but it has quieter weeks, and the buffer of profiles `market/niche.md` keeps
for holidays covers them too.

What would decide how quiet: an operator's monthly call counts across a spring or an autumn. Neither
source gives one.
