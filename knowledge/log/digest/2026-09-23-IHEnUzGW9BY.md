# 2026-09-23 marcus/IHEnUzGW9BY

[Picking a service arbitrage niche used to be hard (until now)](../../references/youtube/marcus/IHEnUzGW9BY/content.md), Marcus Milan. Three trades scored for one city, Bellevue, WA: plumbing, car detailing and pest control, each on demand, competition, job value, repeat/referral and fulfilment, 3 the best. Plumbing came first, pest control second, car detailing third, "consistent with every location". He would skip detailing there for the winters. A pest control profile gets up much easier than a plumbing one. It is the same city as his [2026-10-20](2026-10-20-a7fC901L5ss.md) video, a month earlier.
