## gist

Eric's SEO lesson for profiles: Google ranks the most relevant result, which comes from reviews, authority and keywords, and in his experience review volume and a title with "plumber" plus the city matter most. He finds keywords in a tool, copies a well-ranking non-competing profile's description and replies via ChatGPT, and aims at suburbs where the top three have under 100 reviews and at least 400 searches a month.

### [00:00](https://www.loom.com/share/b497e683ff08477fba2f382082b9aca0?t=0) What ranks

- Google shows what is most relevant to the searcher; the goal is for the profile to be the most relevant for "your service in your city"
  <!--says ?t=0-->
- three factors: reviews, authority, keywords; more good reviews rank higher
  <!--says ?t=0-->

### [02:08](https://www.loom.com/share/b497e683ff08477fba2f382082b9aca0?t=128) Authority, and what he leans on

- authority is whether Google sees the business as authorised for the service; backlinks, such as articles on credible sites saying it is the best plumbing service in Toronto, add signal
  <!--says ?t=128-->
- "from my personal experience", review volume and very specific keywords generate the most leads; deep keyword research is overrated: "if your business has two reviews and you have one location up, no keyword is gonna save you"
  <!--says ?t=128-->
- keyword tools: Semrush (about $200 a month, he uses fresh free trials, "not that useful for GMB"); Keywords Everywhere, a cheap Chrome extension that does the same
  <!--says ?t=128-->
- in a Toronto plumber search a 4.7-rated profile ranked 4th above 5.0 ones: review count and name explain it
  <!--says ?t=128-->
- "your name is the most important part. The title of your business is going to be 60% of the SEO": the most-searched keyword in the city plus the city name
  <!--says ?t=128-->

### [05:36](https://www.loom.com/share/b497e683ff08477fba2f382082b9aca0?t=336) Title from search volume

- in Semrush keyword overview for Canada/Toronto: "plumber Toronto" 2,400 searches a month, "plumbing Toronto" 1,000, around 1,600 for related plumber terms; the most popular keyword in every city is "plumber" plus city name
  <!--says ?t=336-->
- he names every profile "Plumber ... of <city>" (e.g. "Plumber Done Right of Toronto West"); an existing business can rename itself to "Amazing Plumbers and Drain Services of <city>"
  <!--says ?t=336-->
- related terms that carry volume: drain, emergency, 24 hours; these go in title, description and review replies, but "I wouldn't get too nitpicky over exact keywords"
  <!--says ?t=336-->
- description: copy a well-ranked profile that is not competing with you (e.g. "The Honest Plumbers and Drain Services", Dearborn, Michigan, while he is in Toronto) and have ChatGPT redo it with your company name and city; also copy how they reply to reviews and which services they name (hydrojetting, kitchen sink)
  <!--says ?t=336-->
- if you know your industry you do not even need keyword research; put in what people search for in your city
  <!--says ?t=336-->

### [11:28](https://www.loom.com/share/b497e683ff08477fba2f382082b9aca0?t=688) Suburbs are low-hanging fruit

- suburbs have less competition and fewer searches but rank faster; "the top three literally gets like 85% of all calls"
  <!--says ?t=688-->
- Keswick, a Toronto suburb: all plumber terms only about 210 searches a month; top three there had 7, 20, 26, 53, 1 and 39 reviews, and a 509-review profile named "Black Forest" with no plumber keyword or city name ranks anyway; Toronto top results had 330, 113 and 121 reviews
  <!--says ?t=688-->
- his arithmetic: 200 searches, 10% of them calling is 20 calls a month, at a 33% conversion 7 jobs; at a $1,000 average ticket that is $7,000 a month from an untapped area
  <!--says ?t=688-->
- in a dense area like Toronto he could put more profiles; in a small one, one well-ranked profile gets "a ton of results"
  <!--says ?t=688-->

### [15:12](https://www.loom.com/share/b497e683ff08477fba2f382082b9aca0?t=912) The ratio he looks for

- best ratio of competition to volume: at least 400 searches a month and two of the top three pages under 100 reviews; at 400 to 600 searches a month, "get 60 calls a month"
  <!--says ?t=912-->
