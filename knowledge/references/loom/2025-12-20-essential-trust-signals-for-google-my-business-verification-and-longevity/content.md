# Essential Trust Signals for Google My Business Verification and Longevity

- of: [2025-12-20-essential-trust-signals-for-google-my-business-verification-and-longevity.md](../2025-12-20-essential-trust-signals-for-google-my-business-verification-and-longevity.md)

## gist

Eric on the signals that help a page verify and stay up: the Gmail account's age and activity, Search Console, a consistent website, backlinks and citations, the address type, and name variation on redo. He ranks website content most important of these and says that putting up more pages is higher leverage than any of it; his own page on a house, with a broken website, ranks second in its town.

## chapters

### [00:00](https://www.loom.com/share/aa27220453994fcb8edb32b4d024817a?t=0) Account trust and age

- an account that is brand new, bought for a dollar, unaged and without activity is a bad sign. A good one is old with Maps use, bought storage and reviews, a Local Guide level of 4-5: "your mom's account". It raises the chance of instant or email-code verification, and of staying up
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=0-->
- several GMBs owned or managed by one Gmail account get suspended more, anecdotally and not against any policy. It varies by category: detailing pages "very difficult to get suspended", cleaning easy, HVAC pages he has had suspended after two to three months. Use aged accounts of real people (mom, dad, brother) or bought aged Gmails, and send yourself manager access
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=151-->
- in plumbing not every region allows code or instant verification: Canadian plumbing and house cleaning take them; US plumbing is "a lot harder", maybe not possible. So never rely on them. Business video is always "pretty easy" the way he teaches it, and he can get multiple a day
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=151-->

### [04:32](https://www.loom.com/share/aa27220453994fcb8edb32b4d024817a?t=272) Search Console, website, backlinks

- Google Search Console: add the domain and the code Google gives to the hosting or domain settings; "two minutes" and verified on screen. He had never bothered, only worked on clients' pages that had it, but it gives the website credibility when the creation scan reads the domain
  <!--Eric ran 2σ ?t=272-->
- website content: the phone number matches the profile's, the domain matches the profile title and the name appears on the site; more to verify a real business
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=409-->
- backlinks and citations (Facebook, Yellow Pages, MapQuest) can be bought at about a dollar each; he does not think them important, "most people will disagree with me". His `Plumber Done Right of Oshawa` ranked second in the town a day after going up, with no website and no backlinks. Do them after the page is up and only for the shared landing page of a family of pages
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=409-->
- order of weight: reviews, titles, description, then products at the bottom. Pages verified by phone or email code are likelier banned without these set up; "the website content's gonna be the most important" of the trust signals, while more pages and reviews remain "the highest leverage activity"
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=409-->

### [11:41](https://www.loom.com/share/aa27220453994fcb8edb32b4d024817a?t=701) Risky edits, address type, redoing a page

- do not edit the website, name or phone number, nor many edits at once. Logging in from odd places and manager access from different accounts may be flagged: "you can use your common sense"
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=701-->
- houses are cracked down on "pretty quickly", yet Eric has a page on a house (`154 Victoria Street`) that ranks well in a new market, its website going to an error page. Residential apartment buildings are not good either. Large commercial buildings with several units and floors, where the Maps view shows no storefront, do better; a small shop makes it obvious there is no office
  <!--Eric ran 2σ ?t=701-->
- on multiple GMBs at one building some say worse; Eric, from about 50 pages, has found it fine, though many got suspended
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=701-->
- when redoing a page change every factor that did not verify: his Etobicoke page went `...of Etobicoke`, then `...Etobicoke Ontario`, then `...Etobicoke, ON`, since the old one is likely "flagged in the system". No public source says so
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=701-->
