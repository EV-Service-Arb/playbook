## gist

Eric hosts the weekly group call, which runs about an hour and forty minutes, the later part mostly members trading operations. Gabe Jacobs, in car rental, says nearly all his business comes from Google Business Profiles and a marketing agency on top: a client bought 35 profiles that day. Grisha G has built dispatch software on Twilio, GoHighLevel and Stripe where a VA creates a job and the first plumber to text yes gets it; Eric told him to focus on leads before software. A Quebec member with 15 plumbers and a French emergency-plumbing name gets his jobs from two of four or five profiles, quotes emergency calls at $500 minimum, and leaves payment to his plumbers, which leaves him chasing them. Members compared Solo Payments, Stripe and Square, A2P registration on Twilio against GoHighLevel and CallRail, hiring VAs per call, finding plumbers by scraping a licensing board or photographing trucks, and how to talk about not being a licensed plumber. Review stands, paid reviewers, dating-app reviews, live-video verification, bought Gmails and suspension appeals took most of the first hour; those chapters carry a header only.
