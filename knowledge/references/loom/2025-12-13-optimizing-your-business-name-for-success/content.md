## gist

Eric answers Zane's question whether a new page needs a fresh name and domain, as with "detailing". Short answer: probably not, but he does vary both on every new page to be safe, because some pages are suspended on the first go. He shows his own names and a copycat's identical ones on Google Maps.

### [00:00](https://www.loom.com/share/aca8832433c24193ac67c2fbfc6c5724?t=0) Vary the name and the domain per page

- Zane asked whether it is the same as detailing, where you scrap the name and domain for each new page. Eric: "short answer is probably not", but "to be a little bit more on the safe side, I personally would do that and I do do that"

- his pages are `Plumber Done Right of <city>`, with small variations because not all went through first try. Ideally the first submission is published exactly as wanted, `Plumber Done Right of Ajax`; when it is suspended, the next gets a small change
  <!--Eric ran 2σ ?t=41-->
- the changes: add a comma, add the province or state, an abbreviation such as `CA`, a capital letter somewhere, or a comma then the city name
  <!--Eric ran 2σ ?t=71-->
- a rival, `CDP Plumbing`, "instant verifies at the mall" and always uses the same name and the same domain, `cdpplumbing.ca`; Eric thinks he copied him, and sees he sometimes changes it too. His name and domain in Maps: `CDP Plumbing of Scarborough`, `cdpplumbing.ca`
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=97-->
- Eric's domain pattern: one core domain, `PlumberDoneRight.com`, and per city a slash and the city name, "that's the differentiator", for example `PlumberDoneRight.com/Toronto`
