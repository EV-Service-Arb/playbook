# Understanding GMB Rankings and Getting More Calls for Your Business

## gist

Eric answers a student (Ryan) asking how long until a profile starts getting calls. There is no timer: Google ranks for relevance and proximity. An address page gives a proximity edge, but a service-area page ranks across the whole map, and the work that matters is the same either way: description, consistent reviews, replies.

## chapters

### [00:00](https://www.loom.com/share/30cdea04146a4486b4f38eb62544a428?t=0) How long until calls: the wrong question

- there is no set waiting period by page type ("since this is listed as a service area, I'm not gonna rank you for three months"); "All Google cares about is that your page is gonna be relevant to the searchers"
  <!--Eric says ?t=0-->
- an address page is better because a business on an address is one a customer could walk into, and Google puts closer businesses higher
  <!--Eric says ?t=0-->

### [00:48](https://www.loom.com/share/30cdea04146a4486b4f38eb62544a428?t=48) What the address page adds

- the address only gives "that little edge" for people in close proximity; service-area pages "don't have any limitations in terms of where they will rank across the board"
  <!--Eric says ?t=48-->
- Eric's own service-area profile ranks second for "Plumber Vaughan" across Vaughan, though he is not located there
  <!--Eric says ?t=48-->
- address pages may get a few leads with less effort, but top-three competitors there sat at about 35 to 187 reviews
  <!--Eric says ?t=48-->

### [02:18](https://www.loom.com/share/30cdea04146a4486b4f38eb62544a428?t=138) What matters more than page type

- "filling out your description, consistent reviews, replying to your reviews, all of that stuff is what matters most"; matching one of the top three on reviews is what lifts a page
  <!--Eric says ?t=138-->
- an address page with no effort gets "a lead here and there", maybe "a hundred bucks a month", insignificant
  <!--Eric says ?t=138-->
- "a million service area plumbing businesses that do really, really well": Plumber on Demand is big in his city, and its owner keeps offering to buy Eric's profiles "for horrible prices"
  <!--Eric says ?t=138-->

### [03:04](https://www.loom.com/share/30cdea04146a4486b4f38eb62544a428?t=184) Conclusion

- "don't worry so much about whether it's a service area or an address. Worry more about getting these pages ranked"
