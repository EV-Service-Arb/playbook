# Unlocking Live Video Verification for Business Approval

## gist

Eric on the fallback for a business video that was rejected: a live video verification with a human agent, requested by a support ticket. He says that if you can do it you "100%" get approved, and that it is much easier than the recorded video.

## chapters

### [00:00](https://www.loom.com/share/622cc9f153ef4cf2b023ec6e8a21aa8d?t=0) What it is and how to get it

- offered only after a recorded video is rejected; a live agent on a call asks for a few things, where the recorded video is checked by an AI. "Almost always" it ends in approval
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=0-->
- how: search "Google Business Support Form" and submit a help ticket asking for a live video verification agent; once approved, the profile's verification options gain "live video verification", and you are put in a queue
  <!--Eric ran 2σ ?t=67-->
- go early, before about 1 p.m. Eastern: people told him the agent then is lenient and asks little, and later "some strict" one. He calls it "conspiracy", unsure it is real
  <!--Eric heard 0.5σ ?t=67-->

### [02:39](https://www.loom.com/share/622cc9f153ef4cf2b023ec6e8a21aa8d?t=159) What they ask, and spoofing

- they usually ask you to screen-share Google Maps and get directions to the address instead of showing street signs, so a spoofed location "you're gonna get away with this"; sometimes they do not ask for the video but have your location setting on. He refers to location spoofing in an earlier video
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=159-->
- the first live verification can catch you off guard, so have everything legit; after it you know what they ask and can fake it, "maybe even record this on a completely different address"
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=159-->
- typical asks: an invoice, a business card, a dashboard (Stripe, with the text edited by inspect element, "they're not gonna check"), and signage that may be tugged on, so have it somewhat firm. Fake cards and invoices work. "They're not strict at all"
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=215-->
- the same for service-area pages: show everything, and almost every time they approve
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=215-->
