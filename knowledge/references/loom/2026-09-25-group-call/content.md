## gist

Eric hosts the weekly GMB Passive Profits group call; after he wraps up at the hour mark, members
keep talking for another ninety minutes. Members report a mixed week: Dante landed a job two days
after setting up his profiles by finding a plumber on Facebook Marketplace in ten minutes, Lamarr
ranked second after paying students $5 a review on campus, and Derek's Craigslist "review scouts"
are outrunning his supply of profiles, while Sam's plumbers keep no-showing. The group reports almost
every niche now instant-verifying on solid aged Gmails (not plumbing, water damage, locksmith or
garage door in the US), walks Henry through requesting live video verification on screen, and tells
him to appeal a suspension with documents made in Canva. Grisha describes instantly verifying six
Canadian plumbing profiles with aged Gmails, mobile data, a VPN and a location spoofer. The
after-call debates who should take payment, contract terms that keep liability on the contractor,
70/30 versus 60/40 splits, renting leads instead of brokering jobs, AI receptionists, and how to get
reviews outside your own city — David pays $2 a review through Craigslist gigs and leaves them from
aged Gmails behind a mobile proxy and an anti-detect browser, a few a day spread across profiles.
Prabh is to book a one-on-one with Eric, and Eric is to pin a fixed call link so members stop
missing the call.
