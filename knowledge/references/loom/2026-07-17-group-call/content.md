## gist

Eric hosts a weekly mastermind call with students running Google Business Profile (GMB) lead-generation businesses across landscaping, plumbing, and detailing. Cole, Cheyenne, Joseph, Raymond, Ryan, Devin, and others compare subcontractor economics, GMB verification tactics (SAB videos, live-agent video verification, address spoofing), and review-acquisition methods (energy-drink pop-ups, aged Gmail accounts, a paid Reddit reviewer). No formal action items are assigned; each student is expected to apply the tactics discussed to their own profiles.
