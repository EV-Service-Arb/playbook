## gist

A weekly group call in the GMB-arbitrage community, moving freely between plumbing, cleaning, and other service niches as members check in and troubleshoot live. Eric answers most questions as the group's most experienced operator; Devin, Muhammad, Connor, and others share their own numbers and tactics. The dominant threads are Google Business Profile verification (live video now beating AI-spoofed video, which keeps getting flagged), recovering and defending reviews (a Google-support appeal trick that beats older spoofing tools like AdsPower), and the plumber-recruiting grind (commission splits have compressed to 30-35%, with $250+ profit per job as the bar for staying in plumbing). Devin walks through his cleaning-page setup, Eric role-plays two live cold calls to recruit a plumber, and a newcomer, Tony, introduces his videography and tech-support businesses. Next steps discussed: appeal deleted reviews instead of chasing spoofing workarounds, focus profiles on one high-ticket keyword rather than spreading thin, and consider switching from plumbing to cleaning where margins hold up better.
