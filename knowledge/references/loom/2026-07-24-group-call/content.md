## gist

Eric hosts; Luca (detailing in Austin) and Luke (junk removal, insulation) carry the call. Luca reports 10 calls a day plus three or four auto-bookings, a $500 polishing job and a day over $1,000 with six jobs booked for the next day, and a payment scare where a customer left unpaid with a damaged ceramic coat. Luke booked a first $5K job and offers junk-removal subcontractors a flat $50 per lead. The group lands on taking a card on file or a small deposit before the job, and on not letting the crew leave before payment clears. Side deals are on the table: a detailer offered Luca 30% per job on a ceramic coating profile, and another member plans a body-shop profile at 30% of a $10k job with a $20k buyout. High-ticket and out-of-scope calls are handled by referring to another contractor for a commission, as done on a 60/40 HVAC job. Much of the call went to hiring people to collect reviews, tracking them, and verification with AI videos, which are not written up here.
