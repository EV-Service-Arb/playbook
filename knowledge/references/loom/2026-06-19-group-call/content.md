## gist

Eric hosts the weekly group call; about a dozen members speak. James is two months in and had his biggest day, $2,300 to $3,000 with a $1,400 booking; Luca runs about 15 detailing profiles in Austin at around $500 a day; Luke runs junk removal and insulation on one number and cannot find subcontractors at 60/40. Members work out how to tell calls apart in CallRail, whether Nimbata records calls and texts back, and one member has built an AI receptionist for a day of work. A junk-removal operator describes seven years of Google Ads with exact match and a location extension. On splits, a markup of 15 to 20% on larger jobs is relayed from Cole, with a deposit of 30 to 50% from which you pocket your markup, told to the contractor openly. A rank-and-rent operator reportedly takes $2,000 a month per contractor and makes $40k a month. Video verification, suspensions, review collection and aged accounts took a large share of the call.
