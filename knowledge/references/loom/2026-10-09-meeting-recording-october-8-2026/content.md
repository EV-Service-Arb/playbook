## gist

Eric hosts the GMB Passive Profits group call for its first hour, and members carry on for
another forty minutes after he leaves. Reviews take most of it: Henry gets them on campus with a
cooler of drinks, telling students it is his dad's plumbing business; another member seeded a
one-star review on each profile after a big batch and nearly all of the batch stuck; and Gabe
Jacobs shows the pipeline he built with AI that pays $5 a review off Craigslist and Facebook posts,
vetting each person with a form that scores their device, location and timezone against the
profile's city. Eric's answers on profiles: a fresh number and a good gmail for each one, the
website added only once it is up, about three profiles per gmail, and a new gmail instead of
rescuing a bought one that keeps locking out. Others cover a Workspace account as the owner of many
profiles, spoofed Android phones as separate islands, review-getters who cut corners, booking
leads on the spot, and who carries the liability when the money passes through you. After Eric
leaves, Henry and Grisha compare ways to check what a plumber was paid, Valera describes his
tooling and the playbook bot, and Grisha coaches a member through his first live video
verifications. Grisha is to have another profile and 40 reviews on each by next week.
