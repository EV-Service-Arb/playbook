## gist

Eric hosts the GMB Passive Profits group call, which runs on for about forty minutes after he
leaves at the hour mark. Members report that live video verification is passing easily — anything
can be uploaded in the support form to reach the call, and the caller need not be at the location —
and Eric's answer to most questions is the same: stop adding profiles or switching niches and get
reviews on the ones already up. The group trades review methods (a Facebook Marketplace job ad, a
Hinge catfish, free water outside bars, paying hustlers with a bonus per 50 reviews), contractor
sourcing through Facebook construction groups with a roughly 2x markup, and a seasonal HVAC
window-unit removal opportunity. Others cover abandoned GMBs, phone-code and add-a-place
verification, a commission-only sales hire, 1099s, keyword volume for naming an HVAC profile, and
pay-per-call networks to monetize calls before a contractor is found. After Eric leaves, members
compare housecleaning, detailing, concrete and line striping, the missing-place method with
bought Local Guide accounts, and paying a setter per profile. Members are to push reviews this
weekend and reconvene next week.
