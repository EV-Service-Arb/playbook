## gist

This is a weekly group call in Eric's Google Business Profile (GMB) coaching community. Members trade tactics for hiring people to leave reviews (Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace, candy/QR-code incentives), for getting new profiles through phone, email, and video verification without getting suspended, and for picking underused niches (water softening, snow removal, emergency plumbing). A second thread runs through the back half of the call: several members, especially Zach and a water-damage operator, compare pricing models — low monthly retainers versus upfront setup fees plus performance commissions — and discuss owning their own GMB profiles as leverage against client churn. No action items are assigned; Eric closes the call promising to post next week's link earlier.
