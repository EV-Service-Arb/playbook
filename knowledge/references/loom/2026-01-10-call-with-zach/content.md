## gist

Zach Peterson walks a member in Salt Lake City through setting up a Google profile for a commercial cleaning company (Airbnb, post-construction, move-in/out cleaning) that kept getting suspended. Zach runs a large number of plumbing profiles, each on its own Gmail, all listed at addresses. The working parts for operations: forward a CallRail or GoHighLevel number, write a keyword-researched description once, start with 7am to 11pm hours, add the city and service to the profile name, and fill out the page at one edit a day after waiting about five days. Zach's own profiles are listed 24 hours, which has booked him a $600 emergency plumbing job at 11:30 p.m. Verification, suspensions, aged accounts and review collection were the rest of the call and are not written up.

### [22:04](https://www.loom.com/share/c115aafcfb8d45758f67546f05a73bdf?t=1324) Tracking Number, Category and Hours

- Forward calls through a VoIP number: CallRail is about $50 a month with 5 free numbers and $3 for each after; GoHighLevel is about $1.25 per number and includes a CRM and website templates. Zach prefers GoHighLevel but says to stay on CallRail if you are used to it. The member's CRM is Square.
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=1324-->
- Do not use a number you will change, or your personal line, on the profile.
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=1324-->
- Address listings do better than service area ones initially, at the price of harder verification and more suspensions; all of Zach's are on addresses.
  <!--Zach Peterson ran 2σ ?t=1324-->
- Category: checking competitors in the area, commercial cleaners list as "janitorial service", and a second category can be added later. Add custom services such as post-construction cleaning; the more services listed the better.
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=1324-->
- List the hours as 24 hours: it adds activity when others are closed, even if you do not answer. Zach answered a call at 11:30 p.m. and made about $600 on an emergency plumbing job.
  <!--Zach Peterson ran 2σ ?t=1324-->

### [35:25](https://www.loom.com/share/c115aafcfb8d45758f67546f05a73bdf?t=2125) Hours, Description and Keyword Research

- On a new profile start with 7am to 11pm, every day including weekends, since 24 hours at first is a spam flag.
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=2125-->
- Skip the description at setup: do keyword research first, write it once, and avoid editing it.
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=2125-->
- Use SEMrush's free trial or its ten free searches a day. Take four or five keywords and ask ChatGPT for a description of 750 characters or fewer using each two or three times. For "commercial cleaning salt lake" the Keyword Magic Tool showed 53 keywords with total volume 740 and average difficulty 14%.
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=2125-->
  <!--Zach Peterson ran 2σ ?t=2125-->
- Put "commercial cleaning <city>" in the name on later profiles, since it is a major keyword (one named "Wasatch Site Services" lacked it).
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=2125-->
- After about five days begin one edit a day: reviews, then photos, then services and products, then updates; fill in every field with keywords. More profiles are needed because one covers a limited service area.
  <!--Zach Peterson says 1σ ?t=2125-->
