# Creating Effective Service Area GMB Profiles: Step by Step

- of: [2025-12-07-creating-effective-service-area-gmb-profiles-step-by-step.md](../2025-12-07-creating-effective-service-area-gmb-profiles-step-by-step.md)

## gist

Eric's full walkthrough of putting up a service-area profile with a business-video verification, recorded on his own house and a prepared vehicle. He puts the success rate at about 70% and says many profiles can be stacked in one day, each on a new Gmail account, all recorded on the same mailing address.

## chapters

### [00:00](https://www.loom.com/share/fc804298836c4ba282d8002dee239c58?t=0) Why service-area pages

- service-area pages are the safest way to keep a profile up: "pretty much no issues ever", against address pages that may be suspended and then need reinstating
  <!--Eric says ?t=0-->
- they can rank as well as address pages, but take more work to rank. Having several that last beats address pages that keep getting banned
  <!--Eric says ?t=0-->
- name format: the keyword, the company name and the city, e.g. `Plumber done properly of Toronto`
  <!--Eric ran ?t=0-->

### [01:52](https://www.loom.com/share/fc804298836c4ba282d8002dee239c58?t=112) Setup: service area, mailing address, verification choices

- the service area can be set anywhere as long as the video is recorded at the mailing address: Eric, in Toronto, could set an area three hours away and record in Toronto
  <!--Eric says ?t=112-->
- as many pages as wanted in a day: a new Gmail account for each, all recorded on the same mailing address; "you can record 20 in a single day". Not every one succeeds; he sees "around 70%"
  <!--Eric ran ?t=112-->
- Google offers three verifications, email code, phone code and business video. Video is "by far" the most reliable and consistent
  <!--Eric says ?t=112-->

### [04:00](https://www.loom.com/share/fc804298836c4ba282d8002dee239c58?t=240) The video

- Google's own list: show the surroundings (street signs, neighbouring businesses); the business name on cards, a licence or a vehicle; the equipment, booking system or a branded vehicle. It can all be recorded at your house
  <!--Eric says ?t=240-->
- start at the nearest street sign, hold it a couple of seconds and say which intersection and city; commenting "probably does help a little" and "doesn't hurt". "Show everything", hide nothing, keep the phone up, do not skim
  <!--Eric says ?t=240-->
- an unbranded car works: Eric glued a cardstock sign printed for about $10, 12 by 18, to the side with painters tape, with the page's name and a toll-free number. A van is preferable, an SUV is enough. He has not seen them strict on the sign matching the profile name exactly, but "if you can, even better"
  <!--Eric ran ?t=240-->
- walk from the sign to the vehicle showing the houses and their numbers, so the number matches the mailing address
  <!--Eric says ?t=240-->
- at the vehicle: the branding first, from the side and held a couple of seconds; then a walkthrough of equipment. His own was found in the garage, a bucket and a plunger "nothing to do with mold", plus a water-extractor bucket and random chemicals, with commentary on what each is for. His example is for the mold company `Mold Assist`, with many locations
  <!--Eric ran ?t=240-->

### [10:24](https://www.loom.com/share/fc804298836c4ba282d8002dee239c58?t=624) Ending and hours

- "very, very, very important": show yourself physically unlocking the vehicle, with the key or a remote, then start the car and film from inside. The video runs about 2-3 minutes
  <!--Eric says ?t=624-->
- hours: 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. He used to put 24 hours, and it "worked pretty much every single time", but is more careful now because Google can flag it as spammy and more pages are suspending; he is trying to find what causes it
  <!--Eric ran ?t=624-->
- skip the rest of the description and add it later; submit, wait a couple of days, "7 out of 10 times" it goes through
  <!--Eric says ?t=624-->
