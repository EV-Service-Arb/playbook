## gist

Eric's rough-draft plan for building out profiles for a remodeling and restoration contractor in the Salt Lake City area: which category to put in which city by search volume and competition, which locations to run as service-area businesses and which on addresses, an extra `/restoration` page on the website, and tracking numbers on CallRail. The part on how to get the address-based profiles verified is left to the verification notes.
