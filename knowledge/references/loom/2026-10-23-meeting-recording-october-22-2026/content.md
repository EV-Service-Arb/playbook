## gist

Eric hosts the weekly group call, which is mostly about the drop in calls many members have seen
since mid-October. Bradley reports that 26 people answered his post about it; Dante and others find
their profiles out of the top three at every hour. John Osayi puts part of it down to holidays and
local events, and James McBride and Eric point at human activity on the profile: directions requests
from real people nearby, a steady stream of reviews, and engaging with the profile. Eric shares a
member's verification trick of showing an AI-generated street sign on a screen, Dante reports $10k
profit in his first month of plumbing, and Muhammad is talked out of starting on cleaning. The second
half covers getting reviews in person and vetting paid reviewers, blocking spam calls in CallRail,
instant verification, finding contractors, live calls with picky reps, and tools members built for
review collection. Nobody settles what caused the drop; the advice is more reviews, more photos, and
heat map scans to see where a profile actually ranks.
