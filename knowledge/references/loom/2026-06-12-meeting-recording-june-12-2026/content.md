## gist

Eric hosts the weekly group call. Members on it run decks and fences in Ontario (Elliot), plumbing and house cleaning in Virginia (James), and a few newer profiles in Texas and Yorkshire. Elliot has two to three leads a day and no closes yet, takes 15% from one contractor and is trying for 15 to 20% from another. James has one to three leads a day on about 10k search volume, a few $1k days, and says cleaners paid $25 an hour leave 70% of a booking. Eric says VAs should not cover 24 hours, and that Google Sheets beats a CRM until volume is large. The group agrees one contractor per area is unreliable, and argues over a $5,000 retainer for priority leads (too high without proof of lead volume). On pricing, a deposit equal to your markup is pocketed first; a member lost $50k despite three notarized contracts. Eric shows decks and fences, asphalt and landscaping ranking with few reviews in several cities. The CallRail press-one menu is the fix for bot calls. Review recruiting, suspension triggers and aged accounts were also discussed.
