## gist

Valera and Sumit (dainfernalobsidian on discord), both in Eric's group, compare where their
service-arbitrage businesses stand. Sumit, a high-school student in Texas, runs car detailing: a $3/h
VA posting reviews from aged emails kept his ranking profiles up and made about $3K a month, but the
reviews were deleted the next day, so he is going back to getting reviews locally — the mall, the
college — and hiring people for it through Facebook ads. He holds that only keywords and reviews
rank a profile, that six ranking profiles in his city would make $10K a month, and that copying Eric
exactly beats experimenting. Valera, in Clermont-Ferrand, is close to passing live verification —
the step he had been missing is picking a location with no street view coverage — and plans to
start in plumbing with a partner plumber, adding one or two profiles in other niches nearby. Sumit
lists the hurdles still ahead of him: verification, a good VA, and a contractor who carries the
liability; they agree AI call answering waits until $5K a month. They show each other their tooling,
and agree to trade code and connect each other with the people they named.
