## gist

An impromptu group call among service-business owners (plumbing, electrical, insulation, cleaning) trading pricing tactics, GMB/SEO strategy, and lead-gen ideas. TruSpark Electric explains charging by job confidence rather than a fixed 60/40 split and using a credit-card hold instead of a flat inspection fee; Art Khan pushes service fees toward $150-250 and audits members' GMB profiles live, telling Luke (Insulation Done Right, Ottawa) he needs about 70 reviews and 200 backlinks to break the top three. The group also covers niches worth pursuing (fencing, painting, water damage, junk removal), how to sell overflow calls to pay-per-call publishers, and a recent Google core-update rumor. No concrete next steps were assigned beyond members individually testing service fees, backlinks, and reviews on their own profiles.

### [34:19](https://fathom.video/share/Y1jPUhCBxGNW8KjsrwE4P29LC4gbkPYR?t=2059) Selling calls to publishers

- Beyond partnering with contractors, members can monetize excess leads by forwarding calls to pay-per-call publishers (elocal.com, ServiceDirect.com, Ringba.com), which pay per qualified call — e.g. ~$30-50/plumbing call, ~$100/water-damage call — deposited directly, fully passive.
