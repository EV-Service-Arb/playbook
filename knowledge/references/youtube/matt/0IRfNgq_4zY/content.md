# The Real Reason Your GMB's Keep Getting Suspended

- of: [0IRfNgq_4zY.md](../0IRfNgq_4zY.md)
- digested by: `claude-sonnet-5-5`, 2026-11-18

## gist

Matt Gazda, a drop servicing coach (one-on-one offer "0-30k/mo"), answers the question his students and DMs keep asking: why Google Business Profiles get suspended. His frame is trust with Google: a profile with a long good history is never suspended, and he says he can change a profile's name, address and phone freely because of the trust he has. Causes he lists: a brand new or bought email, out-of-pocket profile content, offshore SEO, inconsistent or reused NAP, one domain on several profiles (use subdomains, or separate .com domains, never cheap TLDs), instant or phone-code verification, changing NAP or website domain in the first 6 months, no citations, a shared address, too many negative reviews. Nobody else is on; no scale is stated beyond "students" and "people I know".
