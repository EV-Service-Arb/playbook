# The BEST and WORST niches to hit $20k/mo with Remote Service Arbitrage

- of: [src.md](src.md)
- digested by: /ingest digest, by opus, 2026-11-21

## gist

Eric, who made multiple six figures on plumbing, ranks twelve trades for remote service arbitrage on a
tier list: barrier to entry (how easily the profile verifies, search volume against competition),
ticket size, urgency, recurring revenue, and how easy a contractor is to find. Final board: plumbing,
landscaping, asphalt and paving, deck and fence in GOATED, electrician between GOATED and Amazing;
car detailing, pool cleaning and HVAC between Amazing and Good, house cleaning in Good; pressure washing
and junk removal in Shit; dog walking last. Plumbing is his favourite; detailing the easiest to start.
Results he names: his own plumbing (multiple six figures), Cole on landscaping (multiple six figures,
"hundreds of thousands a month"), agencies selling asphalt and paving leads, Elliot on deck and fence
(quotes of $10–20k out within two weeks).
