## gist

Eric, who runs a remote plumbing business (zero to $100k/mo revenue in two months) and coaches, names
four high-ticket, low-competition niches with a city for each: landscaping in Los Angeles, asphalt and
paving in Atlanta, stucco in Phoenix, masonry in Los Angeles and Toronto. His math for $50k/mo profit:
$7,500 average ticket, 20% margin, 30 jobs, 120 leads a month at a 25% close rate, about four leads a
day — one or two ranked profiles. Competition is read off the map pack (reviews, keyword in the name),
demand off SEMrush, at ~1 lead a day per 1,000 monthly searches. Student results shown: Sami Z's first
close at $9.5k in landscaping 20 days after joining, Cole's $300k landscaping quote, Walker getting
leads daily in asphalt on 3–4 reviews.
