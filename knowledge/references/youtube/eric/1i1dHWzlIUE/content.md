# The Most Profitable Drop-Servicing Niches in 2026 ($300k at 19/yo)

- of: [src.md](src.md)
- digested by: /ingest digest, by opus, 2026-11-21

## gist

Eric, who by 20 has made hundreds of thousands of dollars drop servicing (plumbing now, auto detailing
first), sorts niches into three tiers. High ticket (construction, roofing, renovations, remodels,
landscaping, pools): huge payouts, but a long lead-to-money time, fewer leads, lower close rate,
liability, crews to fulfil, harder setup; his student Nav made ~$80k profit in a month. Mid ticket
(plumbing, HVAC, electrical, pest control decent), his favourite and his own business at a ~$2,000
average: paid the same day, emergencies close at a high rate, one-man jobs, upsells. Low ticket (auto
detailing, cleaning, window cleaning, junk removal, $200–500): paid the same day, recurring, high volume,
easiest setup, but price shoppers and volume needed; he did $15k his second month in detailing. Avoid
pet grooming, window cleaning, pressure washing (no recurrence, low ticket) and anything not a home
service.
