# The Best Drop-servicing Niches to hit 10k/mo

- of: [src.md](src.md)
- digested by: /ingest digest, by opus, 2026-11-21

## gist

Eric, 19, has scaled two drop servicing businesses to $80k/mo revenue — auto detailing (with some
residential cleaning) and now plumbing, which clears him $20–25k profit a month. His three criteria for
a niche: easy to market (competition against search volume in the area: two of the top three under 100
reviews, 400+ monthly searches, ideally 1,000+), high ticket (over $500, so $200+ a booking at 40–50%
margin) and/or recurring (cleaning, pest control, lawn, snow removal), and local, never online. His
pick: car detailing for the first $5–10k a month; past that plumbing, roofing, cleaning and pest control
are about equal, plumbing the default.
