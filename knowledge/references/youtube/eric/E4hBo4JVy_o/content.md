# this niche is slept on, but might be the easiest path to $10k/mo (remote service arbitrage)

- of: [src.md](src.md)
- digested by: /ingest digest, by opus, 2026-11-21

## gist

Eric, who runs plumbing on Google Business Profiles with no ads and coaches students, reveals *HVAC* as
the slept-on niche: his student Jeremiah reached $10,000/mo in 3 months with "not that much work"
(a $2,000+ profit day, 123 calls over a minute in a month, Canada), and Kareem hit 11K this month. Why
HVAC: few lead-gen operators compete in it, unlike plumbing, so incumbents are trade owners without
keywords (Toronto's top profiles carry no city in their names; profiles rank 4th on 11 and 17 reviews);
it verifies by text, phone or email where US plumbing is video only; tickets run from $500 same-day
repairs to $5–10k+ system replacements; it is not seasonal; and its several terms (Phoenix: HVAC
~25,000, AC repair ~10,000, heating and cooling ~4,000) let you pick the best volume-to-competition
ratio. Set-up: verify on an address, not as a service-area business; aim for the lowest review count in
the top three. Calls are booked for a $100–180 diagnostic, waived on the job; no quote off photos.
