# how I build lead gen profiles that print me $30k/mo

- of: [src.md](src.md)
- digested by: /ingest digest, by opus, 2026-11-21

## gist

Eric, who runs mostly plumbing lead-gen profiles in Ontario at a 40% contractor commission and coaches
(around 50 people over 10k a month in nine months, eight or nine over $50,000/mo, by his account), shows
the profile behind most of a $30,000+ month: `Plumber Real Way Newmarket`, 122 reviews done over a few
days a year ago, untouched since, still bringing 1–3 leads a day. Best niches: high-urgency, high-demand
services, plumbing, HVAC, locksmith, garage door repair, closing ~80% of calls, with $8–10k replacement
jobs. The area decides more than anything: at least one or two of the top three under 100 reviews, poor
keyword use by competitors, and 1,000+ relevant searches a month (Thunder Bay plumbing qualifies; one
profile there at ~3% of searches, 50% closed, is ~15 jobs at ~$300 profit a month). Name the profile on
the #1 search, made to sound branded; aim for the lowest review count in the top three (a keyworded
Fairfax VA plumber ranks #1 on 27 over 2,000). On a roofing profile in Saskatoon he fills the
description, services and products with the top keyword via ChatGPT, backdates the opening date, adds a
cheap AI website, and gets ~40 local, unique reviews in a day.
