## gist

Eric, who runs a multi-six-figure remote service business on Google Business Profiles at a 40%
contractor commission, and says he got almost 40 people to 10k a month in 8 months, lays out a game plan
in three parts. Niche type: high ticket (landscaping, asphalt paving, stucco, concrete) is easy to get
leads for but hard to find a contractor for, slow to close and heavy on your involvement; mid-ticket
emergency (plumbing, HVAC) has volume everywhere and easy fulfilment at 40% but more competition and
harder verification, and is his favourite for scaling; low ticket (detailing) pays $100–150 a job to
tire kickers. Locality: do it locally if you can, ~10% easier, since reviews stick only when local; aim
for 1,000+ searches a month (about one lead a day ranked first) and at least two of the top three under
100 reviews (Vaughan plumbing: ~4.9K searches, top three at 47–100; Mississauga: hundreds to 2,588).
Strategy: technical (the best cities, ranked on minimal output) or volume (profiles spread over every
pocket of a city on addresses), which he uses for detailing, as it verifies easily on an address.
