# Picking a service arbitrage niche used to be hard (until now)

- of: [src.md](src.md)
- digested by: /ingest digest, by opus, 2026-11-21

## gist

Marcus Milan scores three niches for one city, Bellevue, Washington (~150k people, ~4,500 per square
mile), on demand, competition, job value, repeat/referral potential and fulfilment, 3 the best. Demand
from `SEMrush` Keyword Magic Tool with company names deselected: plumbing 2,300, car detailing 1,900,
pest control 3,100. Competition from the map pack: plumbing brutal (top profiles in the thousands of
reviews), car detailing medium (~100 reviews could rank first), pest control brutal. Fulfilment from
Seattle contractor Facebook groups: plumbers answer every request (7, 11, 25 comments), a few detailers
advertise, pest controllers are nowhere (Nextdoor or a post of your own instead). Plumbing came first,
pest control second, car detailing third, "consistent with every location"; he would skip car detailing
in Bellevue for its cold, salty winters, and pest control profiles get up much easier than plumbing.
