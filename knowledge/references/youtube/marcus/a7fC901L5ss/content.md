# How I Would Build a $30k/mo Service Business With No Trade Experience

- of: [src.md](src.md)
- digested by: /ingest digest, by opus, 2026-11-21

## gist

Marcus Milan runs plumbing profiles he does not fulfil himself: he takes the call, gathers details, hands
them to an independent contractor and keeps a tiered cut (30% up to $5k, 25% $5–10k, ~20% above $10k,
10–20% discretionary on a $50k job). One example job: $9,400 paid, $7,000 profit, $1,750 to him six days
later. He picks a city by ~500+ searches a month and ~3,000 people per square mile (Bellevue, WA:
~1,000 searches, 4–5k per square mile), and says any industry works: high ticket gives fewer calls and
six-figure quotes, low ticket (cleaning, car detailing) more calls, steadier revenue and easier
verification. His path to $30k a month profit: 12 profiles, an $800 average ticket at 30%, 11 jobs a
month per profile.

### [00:04:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a7fC901L5ss&t=262) picking the industry: high ticket or low ticket

- (!) literally any industry works; there are two schools of thought

### [00:05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a7fC901L5ss&t=327) building the brand and ranking the profile

- once location and industry are chosen: the website, phone numbers, a name, "a brand around this company", then the profile, then verification (covered in his other videos)
  <!--says 1σ ?t=327-->
- once verified, fill every section of the GBP: all services with SEO descriptions, all photos, hours, and service areas of around 10 to 15 zip codes around the profile; build the website around it
  <!--says 1σ ?t=359-->
- then as many five-star reviews as possible, the more the higher it ranks; legitimate customers, friends and family, or otherwise incentivised, "it's all the same to Google", the big companies fight it the same way
  <!--says 1σ ?t=393-->
