## gist

A hair salon in a Thornhill mall has a water heater that heats inconsistently. The answerer books the next morning at 10 a.m. and, asked whether same-day costs more, explains the $60 inspection fee waived on the service. A commercial customer for whom timing is up to the staff, closed in under four minutes.
