## gist

A course screen recording walking a helper through filling in one plumbing profile (Plumber Real Way Newmarket, 154 Victoria St) by copying a competitor reference page and rewording it with ChatGPT: 25 photos, an organic cover photo, 10 products, 10 to 11 Q&As, service descriptions, review replies, twice-weekly updates, about 15 postal codes in a 10 km service area, and a description. Everything is copy-and-reword from a ranking reference page.
