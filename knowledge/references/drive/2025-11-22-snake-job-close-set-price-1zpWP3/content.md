## gist

A landlord from Oakville, with a backed-up basement toilet in North York, calls the first plumber that came up. The answerer is a dispatcher for the business and closes on a flat $250 snake, handling doubts about being a call center, who is coming, and the other listings. The caller does not commit: he wants a callback to confirm the 2:30 slot before driving an hour and a half.
