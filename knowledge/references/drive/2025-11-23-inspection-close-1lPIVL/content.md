## gist

A downtown Toronto tenant wants a drain leak under the kitchen sink fixed and the dishwasher water lines checked before ordering a replacement. The answerer sells one visit for both jobs at a $70 inspection fee waived on repair, gives no repair price before the inspection, moves the time from 5:30 to 6 at the caller's request, and takes the landlord's details to confirm the payer.
