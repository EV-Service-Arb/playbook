### [25:16](https://player.vimeo.com/video/1079014922#t=1516) Rankings and networks

- Q: citations and backlinks still matter beside a full listing? / A: still in the training, but "reviews, and reviews, and number three reviews": have more than every competitor
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=1576-->
- avoid home Wi-Fi when dealing with Google since it ties accounts together; best the phone's data. If uploads need Wi-Fi: a hotspot, or unplug the router for five minutes and check the IP changed. With a proxy on the phone, Wi-Fi is fine, and the same proxy goes on the Firefox profile
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=1690-->
- hosts: Hostgator, "hidden in the crowd"; host nine lets you pick the IP location. Google Search Console only through the same proxy; shared name servers are a footprint only at about a hundred sites. No difference seen between data-centre and residential proxies
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=1860-->
- citations: an individual page per listing (Yelp, Instagram); no shortcut, which is why they outsource it
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=2008-->
- Q: separate sites per location? / A: each location a separate business: its own website, content, design, domain, phone number and name. Seven or eight towns mean seven or eight listings, since you rank only inside the town where you are set up
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=2169-->
- Q: when to use a multi-login app? / A: at about 100 listings, where fingerprinting patterns add up; the recommended settings suit 20-30 listings. At 100, rotate devices for Firefox profiles too
  <!--Eric says 1σ ?t=2237-->

### [38:25](https://player.vimeo.com/video/1079014922#t=2305) Reviews
