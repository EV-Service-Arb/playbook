# reviews
<!--half-life 1y-->

Reviews are built, not waited for: real customers barely review, so the count comes from people we pay or ask, and it keeps coming every week. They come before any tooling, which brings nothing until reviews bring calls. The rest is filed under `reviews/`: sourcing at a [stand](reviews/sourcing/stand.md), through [hired](reviews/sourcing/hired.md) collectors, [online](reviews/sourcing/online.md) and [remote](reviews/sourcing/remote.md); [rate limits](reviews/rate_limits.md), [deletion](reviews/deletion.md), [replies](reviews/replies.md), [local guide](reviews/local_guide.md) accounts.
