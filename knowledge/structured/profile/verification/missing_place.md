## fields

- can I suggest edit, and immediately put site and phone on it?
  A: you can add a lot, but photos are gonna show up as user-submitted + no control over products/services/areas
  <!--2026-09-18 says https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763-->
- (?) "add a missing place" lets you submit: name, category, address/pin, phone, website, opening hours — taking it to a verified profile: `profile/verification/missing_place`
  <!--2026-09-18 says https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763-->
- (?) website and phone both go in at creation time // no need to wait for a claim to attach them
  <!--2026-09-18 says https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763-->
- (?) photos can be uploaded, but land as contributor photos, not owner media
  reason: google decides whether to publish them, and any other user can add their own
  <!--2026-09-18 says https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763-->
- (?) no control over: description, services/products, service areas, logo/cover photo
  reason: owner-only fields, unlocked by claiming + verifying
  <!--2026-09-18 says https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763-->
- (?) the submission is a proposal, not a profile — google may accept the place while omitting, rewriting or later replacing individual fields
  <!--2026-09-18 says https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763-->
- (?) Q: which extra attributes are offered at creation, and which become editable later?
  A: varies by category and region, unspecified

## claiming

- (?) verification never happens at this stage; it triggers when someone claims the listing
  <!--2026-09-18 says https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763-->
- claiming, for categories, description and the rest of the SEO, verifies by text or email code; claimed straight away it is likely suspended within two days
