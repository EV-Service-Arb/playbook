# instant

a profile verifies instantly, with no code, video or live call, on a good gmail: a personal one from the
profile's country, active, which counts for more than its age. the device counts for less than the gmail,
though one that has made many profiles drags each new one down. a failed attempt is retried on another
warmed gmail, the name, address and phone changed slightly, and the tracking number is bought only once
the profile reaches its verification options. which trades verify instantly right now:
[niche](../../market/niche.md#how-it-verifies).
