## on the call

- PP has recovered some pretty bad situations on his live calls: gave the dumbest answers over and over, and got through when the reviewers just got tired. Full conviction and a smile, then outlast them
  <!--2026-10-23 PP ran https://www.loom.com/share/ebbbd994c4a04b8089375226cb930b24?t=5179-->
  <!--2026-10-23 PP ran https://www.loom.com/share/ebbbd994c4a04b8089375226cb930b24?t=5472-->
  <!--2026-10-23 PP ran https://www.loom.com/share/ebbbd994c4a04b8089375226cb930b24?t=5564-->
- (?) once the call is on, don't hang up: meet each ask with a plain excuse, said with conviction — "my internet is down", "the employee took the car", "the cash is in the bank" — and move the camera slowly — `PP`
  reason: the rep wants to approve you but has to be seen applying standards; the call may be audited
