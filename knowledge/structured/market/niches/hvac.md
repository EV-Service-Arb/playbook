# hvac

plumbing's equal on ticket, with few lead-gen operators in it: its top profiles are mostly the
owners', carrying no keyword, so a keyworded address profile, named on "HVAC", ranks quickly wherever a
city is not already held on review counts. most of its calls are small service calls and quotes; a small
repair is done and paid the same day, a system replacement runs to thousands, and the size is in
commercial jobs, bid against others and closed slowly. parts and other trades make a big job revenue more
than profit, so the cut is a fee on top of the contractor's price rather than a split, and the price is
given in the house, never on the phone. the contractor holds the licence to take subcontracted work.
there is work in winter and in summer, less in the mild weather between. it always goes on an address,
and right now instant-verifies on a real person's own gmail, which is why newcomers pick it; a code
google offers is taken only on a real person's own number. it has made a student ~$10k a month within
three months, and a keyworded Toronto profile of Eric's 45 leads its first month. where it is weighed:
[niche](../niche.md#the-starting-trade); its several names are several keywords per city:
[city](../city.md#search-demand).
