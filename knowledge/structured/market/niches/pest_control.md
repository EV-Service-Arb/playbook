# pest control

recurring and urgent both: customers on monthly maintenance plans, and emergency jobs wanted gone at
once. it is among the most searched trades, and its competition is as heavy as plumbing's. profiles get
up without a video, easier than plumbing's though harder than most trades'. the contractor is the hard
part: few advertise. in some places it makes less in winter, so it runs in a warm city. Eric counts it
among the best trades to get into; Marcus Milan ranks it second to plumbing. where it is weighed:
[niche](../niche.md#recurring-work).
