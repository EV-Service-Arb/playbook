# pool cleaning

recurring by nature: each new customer stays on and adds to the month, where detailing and cleaning
only can recur. a weekly visit is worth little after the cleaner is paid, but a customer is worth
thousands over the years, so the money is in customers adding up, on many more calls than plumbing and
with a VA early. competition is low: a keyworded profile tops Dallas on 26 reviews. profiles verify
instantly on a good gmail. in a cold city it dries up in winter, so it runs in a warm one. how
recurring work weighs against the ticket: [niche](../niche.md#recurring-work).
