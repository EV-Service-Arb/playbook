# niche

we start on plumbing, or on drain cleaning under it: urgent, year round, high ticket for the same
setup cost, and any plumber fulfils it. a low-ticket trade such as detailing pays soonest but takes many
more jobs for the same money; recurring work sits between. always a local service. an untapped trade is
found by searching a sub-service by its own name. one trade is mastered before a second is added. each
trade's own page is under [niches/](niches/).
