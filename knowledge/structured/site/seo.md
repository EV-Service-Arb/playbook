# seo
<!--half-life 1y-->

The site ties the profile to its searches, and needn't be good to do it: a call to action, a request
form, and the profile's services with matching descriptions. a profile ranks without one; a site alone
brings nothing for months. it carries the service and the city in one H1 per page, and gives each
location its own hand-written page.
