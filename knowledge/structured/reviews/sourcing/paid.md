# paid

strangers paid a few dollars each to leave one review of their own, found off an ad, as against
[hired](hired.md) collectors who go out for them. the ad is a small job on craigslist or a small
business's post on facebook, worded around the platform's trigger words, and all talk moves off the
platform to the phone. each respondent leaves one review on one profile; their friends and family count
only as people of their own. it is slower than paying for volume, and what it gets sticks.
