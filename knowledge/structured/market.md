# market

we pick the trade on what the job is, then the city to run it in on what the market there is, and
rank organic profiles rather than buy ads. the trade: [niche](market/niche.md), each trade's own page
under [niches/](market/niches/). the city: [city](market/city.md).

## how a market is judged

- (?) the job first — urgency, ticket, how easy it is to fulfil, margin, seasonality — then the city: competition and search demand
  - a niche is judged on the service — urgency, ticket, how easy it is to fulfil, margin, seasonality — then on the market it is run in: competition and search demand — Eric
