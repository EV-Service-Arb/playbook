# contracts
<!--half-life 5y-->

before his first job we agree with him how jobs get quoted and what our cut is. he stays the contractor, never a partner quoting as ours: we make money as the liaison. every contractor signs an independent contractor agreement: it requires his licenses and certifications and makes him liable for the work he does; where he collects, it leaves the fee and the customer with him, and where we collect, it lays the job's blame on him. the text is a standard independent contractor agreement for the trade, and it can wait until he depends on our jobs.
