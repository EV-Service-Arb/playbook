**Advice**

- **Default to a physical address.** Every ranking source agrees [1][3][4][5], and Eric repeats it [2]. The backing is moderate, with norms around 0.45–0.54. Most of it is unvetted opinion, not controlled data. Source 5 is the only one that comes from members' own numbers.
- **Plumbing is where it matters most.** Source 5 says you need the address shown for plumbing. It also says some niches, such as drain cleaning, can get by with a service area. That is one unscored voice, so treat it as a hint.
- **Service area is the cheaper way in, with a ranking cost.** It's easier to get verified and can be recorded from home [3][6]. You pay for that with weaker ranking and fewer leads [1][4], and the 50% success rate in [7] is a rough anecdote, not a benchmark.
- **A hybrid route exists.** Create the profile as a service area, then convert it to an address [8]. In the Canada case it was instant with no video. It's moderately backed (norm 0.45), and the same method hasn't been tried in the US. Members from the UK report struggling to convert service-area profiles to physical ones in the US (2026-09-11 group call, unscored).
- **What I can't tell from your question:** your niche, whether you have a real or rentable address, and your country. If you have a legitimate office, go straight to an address. If you don't, you're choosing between two options:
  - Use service area and accept weaker rankings.
  - Spend effort on address video verification or conversion, which has more failure risk.
- Split-testing the two on a few profiles is what the group itself concluded in the 2026-07-24 call.
