## summary

- Run a local rank map to see Google Maps positions; the top three receive about 80% of service-search calls.
- Create and optimize a Google Business Profile at business.google.com, connect a matching website, and consider a second profile about 10 miles away.
- Build review quantity and weekly review velocity using tap cards or post-job automations.
- Use a verified address, correct primary and additional categories, and matching website title/H1 tags with one H1 per page.
- Publish original geographic content with landmarks and local details, and include the main service and location in the business name when possible.
