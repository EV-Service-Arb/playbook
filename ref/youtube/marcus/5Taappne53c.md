## summary

- Choose remote GBP locations with at least 30,000 residents, ideally 50,000–300,000, and density of about 6,000 people per square mile.
- Target areas with minimum median income of $70,000, ideally $90,000–$130,000, while avoiding low-income and high-crime neighborhoods that produce poor leads and contractor safety issues.
- Evaluate competition by Google profile reviews: 50–100 or fewer reviews is easy, 100–200 is medium to hard, and 300-plus is generally not worth targeting.
- Confirm at least 400 relevant monthly searches, excluding business-name searches, using SEMrush; then ensure a contractor can service each profile within roughly 45–60 minutes.
