## Conclusion

2–6 profiles per managing gmail, set by how much one ban would take with it:

- up to 5–6: we are added as manager, opsec is clean, and the managing email is high-trust and on an
  org (workspace)
- 2–3 at most: we sit on the profiles as owner, or the account is already tainted

The numbers never conflicted as much as they read: Eric's 3–5 answered a question about owner seats on
profiles strangers made, his 2–3 was general, and on 10-09 he says there is no concrete number.
Nobody tested a limit; one-per-email is a member's preference, cheap on a workspace. What decides the
range is what links profiles and how trusted the holder is: manager over owner is Eric's own rule, and
a high-trust org account is the owner's call.
