# recordings

A transcript misses what was only shown: a dashboard, a count, a form. That reading lives beside the
capture, per [Layers](../../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#layers).

- a recording is reached only through `scripts/call-pull.rs --recording <capture.md>`, kept or fetched
- `shown.md`, or a youtube capture's `## shown`, is a model's reading. Open the frame a line embeds
  before leaning on it
- a claim only the picture makes cites that line's second
- `scripts/call-watch.rs` spends money per recording, and with no arguments takes every unwatched one
  under `ref/`. Name the captures. `cargo r -- re-transcribe picture <path>` prices a redo
