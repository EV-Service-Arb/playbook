# notes

The owner's notes and chats, line by line. Placement and format are [distill](../distill/SKILL.md)'s.

- the owner's own words go to `approved/`, with `// unsourced: Valera`. Once a capture holds them, the
  line is replaced by the citation of the second they were said
- someone else's words in a chat are their claim, at their base, into `suggested/`
- a person: [people.md](people.md)
- about us, not the market: [internal.md](internal.md)
- a todo you can do, do. Only what you can't is filed
