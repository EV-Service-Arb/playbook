# resolve

Sources may disagree; `structured/` may not. Run when it contradicts itself, or a source contradicts it.

- gather every mention first, across `structured/` and `ref/`
- then weigh: reliability and age, what each speaker meant, how sure they sounded, what they were doing
  when it worked or failed. The rest of what we know on the topic counts, and so does common industry
  knowledge where the practice overlaps
- the conclusion is our opinion. Where the evidence is not conclusive, make it less precise rather than
  pick a side: some say 2–3 profiles per gmail at most, others run 5–6, so the claim is `2–6*`, with what
  moves a case along that range
- the record, `secondary/resolved/<date>-<slug>.md`: every source weighed, linked, and a short why for
  the conclusion
- the conflicting bullets become one, with a `resolved:` line linking the record
- when `approved/` is involved, the record goes to `tmp/resolved/` and the conclusion to the owner.
  Apply it only once they confirm
