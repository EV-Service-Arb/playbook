# integrate

Carry out one proposal from `knowledge/log/digest/` in the manual, in
[the page format](../../knowledge/README.md#manual-structured).

The page is the unit, not the bullet. For each page the proposal names, read the whole page, then
decide what it should say now:

- evidence for a conclusion already there joins it. Never a second bullet for one claim
- a conclusion the new evidence qualifies is rewritten to say so
- a step the page lacked is placed where it is met, not at the end
- a section collecting two subjects splits; a page collecting two splits into children
- the lead is rewritten whenever what we do changed

Then read the page top to bottom as someone who has never seen the call. It reads as one text, or it is
not done.

- everything you write opens `(?)`. A bullet without one is the owner's: move it, put it under a new
  `(?)` conclusion, never change its words and never mark it
- a conclusion says what we do or what is so. `Dante found a contractor in ten minutes` is evidence;
  `facebook marketplace finds a contractor within the hour` is the conclusion it stands under
- a contradiction: [resolve.md](resolve.md), never two bullets. Two numbers for one quantity are not one: both stay, as evidence
- the proposal's `## not kept` stays not kept
