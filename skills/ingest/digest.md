# digest

Write `<capture>/content.md`: what the recording says, as chapters of attributed points, in
[the digest format](../../knowledge/README.md#digest-contentmd). It is the only way anything in the
recording reaches the manual, so what it leaves out is lost.

## Before the capture

Read these, so you know what we would act on and whose word weighs:

- the manual's outline: `find knowledge/structured -name '*.md'`, and the lead of the pages nearest the
  recording's subject
- [people](../../knowledge/README.md#people): who runs what, and at what scale

## Passes

A single read of a two-hour call loses its middle. Three passes:

1. **segment.** Go through the capture stamp by stamp and mark where the subject changes. A chapter
   is one subject: one member's update, one question and its answers. Tens of them for a long call
2. **read each chapter**, with its lines of `shown.md` beside it, and write its points
3. **`## gist` and `## open`, last**, from the chapters. Then merge a point two chapters both made

## A point

- what was said, in the speaker's terms. Numbers, prices, counts, names, handles and tool names exactly
  as said: `$5 a review`, `11 profiles`, `Octobrowser`
- who said it, from what is said: names, roles, who answers whom. Loom names no speakers. A speaker you
  cannot name is their group's row, `gmbpp member`
- how they know: `ran` — they did it and report what happened; `says` — they assert it; `heard` — they
  pass on someone else's
- a point only the picture makes cites the second of its `shown.md` line. Open the frame before leaning
  on it: `shown.md` is a model's reading
- a question and what it was answered is one point, `Q: … / A: …`, cited to the answer
- a mis-transcribed name is written right, with what the transcript has: `Magisk` // transcribed "Majestic"

## What is kept

Keep what would change what we do, or what we expect when we do it: a method, a number, a result, a
failure and what it cost, a tool, a price, a rule of Google's as someone met it. Drop greetings,
scheduling, and a claim restated.

Weigh the speaker. Someone running tens of profiles, a team or real revenue gets every specific they
give written down; a newcomer's guess gets a line if it gets one.

When the owner's own notes of the call exist, every note has a point.

## Mistakes

- a stamp the capture does not have. Copy stamps; never compute one
- a point the recording does not make: our conclusion, a generalisation, advice
- naming a speaker on a guess. The group's row is always available
- touching the capture
- five chapters for a two-hour call
