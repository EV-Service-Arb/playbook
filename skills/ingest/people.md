# people

Adding or updating a person is `/rolodex`'s, under the `ServiceArb` tag.

- the [people](../../knowledge/README.md#people) table is fed from the rolodex, never written first. When
  someone helps or shares, raise their `available`; when they ignore an ask, lower it
- a new speaker gets a row standing in for their group. A trust of their own is the owner's to set.
  Ask; never derive one from how much they've said
