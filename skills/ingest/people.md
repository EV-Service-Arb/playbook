# people

Adding or updating a person is `/rolodex`'s, under the `ServiceArb` tag.

- the [experts](../../guides/experts.md) guide is fed from the rolodex, never written first. When someone helps or shares,
  raise their `available`; when they ignore an ask, lower it
- a [people](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#people) base is the owner's to set. Ask; never derive one from
  how much they've said. Once it changes, every citation of theirs is rewritten to it
