# internal

What is about us: how the team runs, what we decided, what we owe each other. Members never see it.

- a todo becomes an issue: in the repo of the tool it concerns, else this one, else `EV-Service-Arb/.github`
  when no repo fits. Labels per the `v-flakes` skill. Assign `valeratrades` when only the owner can act
- what we know about our own operations goes to `knowledge/internal/`, in the page format. Its topics
  come from what arrives, none made ahead
- our team's recordings never go through the pullers: they file under `knowledge/references/<platform>/`, and `knowledge/references/` is
  baked into the member image. What they say enters cited with no url
- nothing read from `knowledge/internal/` lands in `knowledge/structured/`
