# notes

The owner's notes and chats, line by line. Placement and format are [integrate](integrate.md)'s.

- the owner's own words enter without `(?)`, cited `<!--<date> Valera says-->`. Once a capture holds
  them, the citation gains the second they were said
- notes of a call that has a capture go to its [digest](digest.md) first: every note has a point there
- someone else's words in a chat are their claim, cited to them with no url, and open `(?)`
- a person: [people.md](people.md)
- about us, not the market: [internal.md](internal.md)
- a todo you can do, do. Only what you can't is filed
